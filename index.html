NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Some platform features are still under development. We’ll clearly identify what is live before accepting payments or orders.

Is WESTBRIDGE SMM accepting orders now?+

No. This is the first-stage website preview. Accounts, payments and order fulfilment must be implemented and tested before launch.

Which currency will the platform use?+

The planned display and billing currency is US dollars (USD), subject to payment-provider availability and applicable requirements.

Will orders be processed automatically?+

The planned system supports both authorised provider integrations and administrator-managed processing. Each integration will be tested before activation.

Do I need to share my social media password?+

No. Customers should never provide their social media passwords to place a promotion order.
